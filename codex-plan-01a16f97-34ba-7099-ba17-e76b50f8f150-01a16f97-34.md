# Android Medicine Reminders with Prescription Setup

## Summary

Build an English-language Android app for your mother and you. Use **both prescription pages** to prepare medicine drafts, organize reminders around her meals, and notify you **15 minutes after an active scheduled dose remains unconfirmed**.

## Prescription and schedule setup

- Prepare private draft entries from both pages. Preserve the written instructions and flag unclear names, strengths, units, or directions for confirmation with her doctor or pharmacist.
- Keep reminders off until each medicine’s details and schedule are confirmed.
- Ask for her usual breakfast, lunch, dinner, and bedtime times, plus her timezone. Do not assume clock times or before/after-meal intervals.
- For each routine medicine, confirm the dose, meal relationship, exact reminder time, treatment start date, and prescribed duration.
- Support fractional tablets and liquid doses without converting unclear units.
- Keep as-needed medicines available for logging without automatic overdue alerts. Show dialysis injections, infusions, and catheter treatments separately as clinician-administered reference entries.
- Stop future reminders when a confirmed treatment course ends. Schedule changes affect future doses and preserve history.

## App experience

- **Mother’s screen:** large text, today’s medicines, the next reminder, and prominent **Taken** and **Snooze 5 minutes** controls.
- **Your screen:** review prescription drafts, manage schedules, and see dose confirmations and recent history.
- Snoozing preserves the original family-alert deadline.
- Family notifications say “This dose hasn’t been marked as taken.” When confirmation may be delayed by lost connectivity, explain that clearly.
- Provide notification-permission guidance, reminder-readiness status, and a test reminder during setup.

## Implementation and interfaces

- Build with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose; use Room for offline schedules and pending confirmations.
- Use Android alarms for local reminders and restore schedules after phone restarts.
- Use Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for private synchronization and family alerts.
- Link your accounts through a single-use invitation code; restrict access to the linked family.
- Store prescription drafts, medicine instructions, meal anchors, confirmed schedules, individual dose occurrences, confirmations, and notification tokens.
- Give every dose a unique identifier to prevent duplicate records and alerts.
- Check overdue confirmations every minute; send one family alert per eligible dose after 15 minutes.
- Store prescription information as private user data, outside the app source and distributable APK.

## Tests and delivery

- Verify prescription review blocks activation of incomplete or unclear entries.
- Test meal-time changes, fractional doses, course end dates, midnight boundaries, and timezone changes.
- Confirm as-needed and clinician-administered entries never generate overdue family alerts.
- Test offline reminders, delayed synchronization, restart recovery, denied permissions, snoozing, and duplicate prevention.
- Verify account isolation and notifications on two Android phones.
- Deliver an installable APK and setup instructions. Live family alerts require a configured Firebase project with billing enabled for scheduled functions.

## Defaults

- One mother and one family member; both use Android.
- English interface, meal-based setup, and a 15-minute family-alert delay.
- No automatic dosing decisions, SMS, or Play Store publication in the first version.
